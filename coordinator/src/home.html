How a label is decided
  1. Three different nodes answer, each with a random Llama model and instruction style.
  2. All three agree: kept.
  3. They don't: a large judge model gives one hint (never the answer), three fresh votes try again, and only unanimous answers are kept.
  4. Hidden honeypots with known answers catch cheating nodes, which get quarantined.
