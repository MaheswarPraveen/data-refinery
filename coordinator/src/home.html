Kept as evidenceEvery vote stays in the ledger and in each item's exported record, marked as disagreeing.

Research foundation

OpenAI · Burns et al., 2023 · arXiv:2312.09390
Weak-to-Strong Generalization
ShowsA strong model trained on labels from weaker models can outperform those weaker supervisors.
For usOur small models are the weak supervisors. Consensus filtering gives the stronger model cleaner weak labels to learn from.
Google · Wang et al., 2022
Self-Consistency

Majority vote over several reasoning paths beats any single answer. We vote across different models instead of one.
Google · Huang et al., 2022
LLMs Can Self-Improve

Training on high-agreement answers improves the model. Our accepted items are exactly that kind of data.

Accuracy grows with the number of voting agents. The basis for scaling to many volunteer nodes.

Anti-cheating and integrity9 safeguards · honeypots, HMAC-SHA256 signatures, timing checks · click to expand

Nodes are strangers' machines, so every vote is treated as untrusted until it is verified.

Honeypot questionsa few test items with known answers, hidden among real data; a node below 30% is quarantined
HMAC-SHA256 signaturesevery answer is signed with the node's secret key; forged or altered votes are rejected
Blind votingnodes only receive the question, never other answers, so they can't copy
Timing checksanswers faster than the model could compute are discarded as fake work
One vote per node per itemleases and duplicate checks stop a node from voting twice
Judges can't decidesigned hints only, with answers and letters scrubbed out automatically
Model diversityten model families, so no single model's blind spot can win
Credit for agreeing with consensusa vote earns credit when it matches what the network accepted. No answer key needed
Full audit trailevery signed vote is stored, so any result can be recounted
