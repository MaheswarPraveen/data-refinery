How to read this

This is a test of label quality, not a trading tip.

Nodes see only the headline and the company name, never the price. Returns are filled in from real market data after a label is settled.

If the consensus labels are meaningful, positive headlines should beat the index more often than negative ones. A gap near zero means the labels carry no price signal.

Up / down = beat or lagged Nifty by more than 1%. Past headlines only; earlier trading tests on this data never beat random picks.
