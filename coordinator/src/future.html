Every idle GPU becomes part of the refinery.

The plan: anyone can contribute a home PC as a node, larger GPUs act as judges, dataset owners fund a reward pool, and contributors are paid for verified work.

question out signed answer back split item → hint accepted item reward

Who does what

Regular node
anyone with a 2–4 GB GPU
  • Runs small models while the PC is idle
  • Answers questions independently
  • Paid when its answer agrees with the network's consensus
Judge node
24 GB+ GPU owners, labs
  • Runs a 30–70B model
  • Writes hints for split items, never answers
  • Paid per hint that leads to agreement
Dataset owner
model builders, researchers
  • Uploads data to refine or verify
  • Funds the reward pool
  • Pays only for accepted items
Coordinator
open, replicated
  • Assigns jobs, enforces the rules
  • Publishes every signed vote
  • Anyone can audit the result
