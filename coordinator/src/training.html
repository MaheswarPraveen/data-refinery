Why refined data beats regular data

Regular datasets carry hidden mistakes, and models learn them. The refinery removes them.

+3.6points: refined vs raw data, same model and problems (Llama 8B)
+4.0points: refined vs GSM8K's own human-written answers (Llama 8B)
30% → 2%wrong labels before and after refining (Error Detection Test)

Even well-known benchmark datasets contain wrong labels (MIT, "Pervasive Label Errors", 2021); scraped data is worse. Cleaning by hand costs ₹10–50+ per item; the refinery does it for about ₹2–6 with volunteer GPUs. Gains are largest on messy data and grow with stronger voter models.
